    How to measure

    Bust
    Around the fullest part, with the tape level across your back.
    Waist
    Around the narrowest part, just above your navel.
    Hip
    Around the fullest part, with your feet together.
    Length
    From the top of your shoulder down to where the hem should sit.

    Measure over light clothing. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one or ask Supplier Hub on WhatsApp.
